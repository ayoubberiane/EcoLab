# EcoLab | The Interactive Economics Laboratory

**Understand the economy. Change the variables.**

ÉcoLab is an interactive educational platform designed to make economics more accessible, practical, and engaging. It combines economic theory, data analysis, interactive simulations, and calculation tools to help students understand how economic systems work.

Rather than simply presenting definitions and formulas, ÉcoLab encourages users to experiment with economic variables, visualize relationships, solve problems, and explore real-world economic scenarios.

## 1. Project Vision

Economics is not just about understanding theories. It is about understanding the relationships between individuals, businesses, industries, institutions, and entire economies.

ÉcoLab aims to transform the learning experience through three interconnected levels of economic analysis:

- **Microeconomics:** Understanding individual economic decisions.
- **Mesoeconomics:** Understanding industries, sectors, markets, and regional economic structures.
- **Macroeconomics:** Understanding the economy as a whole.

The platform connects these three perspectives to demonstrate how individual decisions can influence industries and ultimately shape national economic outcomes.

## 2. The Three Economic Laboratories

### 01. Microeconomics — Individual Decisions

Explore how consumers and firms make decisions and interact in markets.

Topics include:
- Supply and demand
- Market equilibrium
- Price elasticity
- Consumer theory and utility
- Production and costs
- Revenue and profit
- Opportunity cost
- Marginal analysis
- Market structures and competition

### 02. Mesoeconomics — Industries and Economic Systems

Explore the structures connecting individual firms to the wider economy.

Topics include:
- Industrial organization
- Value chains and value added
- Industrial clusters and ecosystems
- Market concentration
- Sector productivity
- Regional economic development
- Supply chains
- Inter-industry relationships

### 03. Macroeconomics — The Economy as a Whole

Understand national economic performance and the factors influencing economic activity.

Topics include:
- Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
- Gross National Income (GNI)
- National income and accounting
- Consumption and investment
- Savings
- Inflation and price indices
- Unemployment and employment
- Economic growth
- Fiscal and monetary policy
- Exports, imports, and trade balance

## 3. Interactive Features

ÉcoLab is designed around learning by doing.

Planned and developing features include:

- **Economic Simulations:** Experiment with supply, demand, prices, and market equilibrium.
- **Economic Calculators:** Calculate elasticity, GDP, market concentration, and statistical indicators.
- **Data Visualization:** Explore economic relationships through interactive graphs.
- **Learning Modules:** Understand concepts through explanations, formulas, and practical examples.
- **Knowledge Checks:** Test understanding through exercises and quizzes.
- **Cross-Level Analysis:** Explore how microeconomic decisions connect to mesoeconomic structures and macroeconomic outcomes.

ÉcoLab is developed using lightweight web technologies:

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| HTML5 | Website structure |

## 5. Getting Started

### Run locally

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/ecolab.git
   ```

2. Navigate to the project directory:

   ```bash
   cd ecolab
   ```

3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

### Deploy with GitHub Pages

1. Upload the project files to your GitHub repository.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Select **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)` directory.
5. Save your settings.

Your website will be published through GitHub Pages.

## 7. Educational Approach

ÉcoLab follows three principles:

1. **Understand:** Learn the economic concept and its theoretical foundations.
2. **Experiment:** Change variables, perform calculations, and observe outcomes.
3. **Connect:** Understand how economic phenomena interact across different levels of analysis.

The objective is to move beyond memorization and encourage economic reasoning, analytical thinking, and practical problem-solving.
