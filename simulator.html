HOW TO READ IT

What the shapes mean.

Every coloured area on the chart is a quantity of welfare you can measure.

Consumer surplus

The gap between what buyers would have paid and what they actually pay, summed over every unit bought.

Producer surplus

The gap between the price sellers receive and the lowest price they would have accepted.

Tax revenue

The tax per unit times the quantity still traded. It moves surplus to the government; it is not lost.

Deadweight loss

Trades that no longer happen because of the tax. This surplus goes to nobody, so it is the true cost.

TRY THIS
  1. Set a tax of 20, then make demand steep. Who pays most of the tax now?
  2. Keep the tax and make both curves flat. What happens to the deadweight loss?
  3. Raise the tax until the market shuts down. Where does all the surplus go?
